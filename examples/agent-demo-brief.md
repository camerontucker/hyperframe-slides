# Team update

Audience: staff preparing a 10-minute meeting.

Purpose: show how an agent can draft a presentation while the presenter keeps editorial control.

Required points:

- The agent turns a short brief into an editable local deck.
- The presenter reviews the slides and speaker notes before sharing the audience window.
- A visual review should catch clipping or overlap before the meeting.

Style: calm paper theme, three slides, shared header and footer, one idea per slide.
